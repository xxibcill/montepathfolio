# Portfolio Risk Sandbox

Portfolio Risk Sandbox describes simulated portfolio outcomes and the risk
measures used to compare them.

## Language

**Tail capital shortfall**:
The average loss relative to contributed capital among the lowest five percent
of terminal portfolio values.
_Avoid_: Expected Shortfall, CVaR

**Expected Shortfall**:
The average loss beyond a selected Value at Risk threshold under the documented
loss convention.
_Avoid_: Tail capital shortfall
